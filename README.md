## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 1. LocalMind AI — Offline AI Chatbot
A Flutter-based mobile AI chatbot designed to run language models directly on the device.

- Explores local LLM inference using GGUF models and `llama.cpp`.
- Supports a model download and selection workflow.
- Focuses on offline access, efficient inference, and reduced response latency.

**Tech:** Flutter, Dart, GGUF, llama.cpp, On-Device AI

🔗 [View Repository](https://github.com/ArkhadeSarthak/offline-ai-chatbot)

### 2. Janarakshak AI — Indian Legal AI Assistant
An AI-powered legal assistance platform designed to make legal information easier to understand and access.

- Designed around legal question answering and document retrieval.
- Planned capabilities include legal references, multilingual assistance, and AI-powered explanations.
- Envisions government scheme discovery and legal document generation.

**Tech:** React, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Vector Databases, Generative AI

🔗 [View Frontend Repository](https://gitlab.com/ai-team2026/janarakshak-ai-frontend)
🔗 [View Backend Repository](https://gitlab.com/ai-team2026/janarakshak-ai-backend)

### 3. RAG AI Chatbot
A document-based AI chatbot concept that uses retrieval to provide context-aware answers from user-provided documents.

- Document ingestion, chunking, and embedding generation.
- Semantic search using a vector database.
- LLM-powered answer generation grounded in retrieved context.

**Tech:** Python, LangChain, RAG, Embeddings, ChromaDB/Qdrant, FastAPI, React

🔗 [View Repository](https://github.com/ArkhadeSarthak/Personal-AI-ChatBot)

### 4. Multilingual AI Voice Calling App
A Flutter-based application concept for real-time multilingual voice communication.

- Designed for callers to speak and listen in their preferred languages.
- Explores speech recognition, translation, and voice delivery.
- Uses a real-time communication architecture with a Python backend.

**Tech:** Flutter, Python, FastAPI, LiveKit, Redis, AWS

🔗 [View Repository](https://github.com/ArkhadeSarthak/AI-Calling)
